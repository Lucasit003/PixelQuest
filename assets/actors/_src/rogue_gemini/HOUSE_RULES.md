# The house style, measured off the two APPROVED characters

Read off the art, not described from memory. These are what make the Rogue look
like the third character by the same artist rather than one generated from a
text prompt.

|  | Warrior | Paladin | rule for the Rogue |
|---|---|---|---|
| figure height | 184px src | 215px src | **215px** — same |
| heads tall | 5.8 | 5.0 | **~5.0–5.5**, adult, never chibi |
| chin at | 17% | 20% | **~20%** of height |
| head width | 54px | 53px | **~53px** — the SAME head, all three |
| head as % of height | 29% | 24% | 24–29% |
| shoulder span | 123px* | 87px | **< 87px** — narrower than plate armour |
| shoulder / height | 0.67* | 0.40 | **~0.33** |
| outline | 2–3px | 1px | **1px** (Paladin is the newer, cleaner reference) |
| mean colour run | 1.01px | 1.04px | **~1px** — true pixel art, no chunky blocks |
| lighting | upper-left | upper-left | upper-left |
| equipment | separate sprites | separate sprites | separate, `mainHand` + `offHand` |

\* the Warrior figure is measured ARMED, so his 123px span includes sword and
shield. His body alone is narrower than that number suggests.

## The one number that matters most

**Shoulder span.** The Paladin is 96px wide at 215px tall while wearing full
plate. Any Rogue wider than that has failed before anything else is judged,
because the silhouette read — lean / agile / precise — is carried almost
entirely by width.

## v1 verdict: REJECTED

140px wide at 215px tall. **Broader than the armoured knight.** Craft was good —
correct 5.0 heads, correct 29% head, right palette family, right outline — but
the silhouette was wrong, which is the thing that cannot be fixed later.

Specific faults: arms held out wide from the body; cloak flaring sideways rather
than hanging; hood too large and round; roughly double the Paladin's strap and
buckle detail; a dagger and sheath at the belt when the body must be permanently
weaponless.
